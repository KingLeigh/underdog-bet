- [ ] **Explore a pari-mutuel betting toggle on the Setup screen** — instead of proposer-set odds, all bets on a wager form a pool and the winning side splits it pro rata (payout multiplier = total pool ÷ winning side's pool; the side with less money is the underdog by construction). Self-correcting market: distorting the odds requires staking chips on the side you think loses. Design notes from the 2025-11 discussion: run an **open tote** — sides and running totals visible live while betting is open (the closed-tote variant creates a hidden-side "double bluff" guessing game, rejected); the live board makes the odds a visible moving price, and herding is self-punishing because piling onto a side shortens that side's own payout; bets are final once placed (raise-only) and the host counts down the lock to blunt buzzer-sniping; a live tote board is a natural fit for the planned presentation screen; seed each pot with a house stake (~20) so unanimous rounds still pay and contrarians are subsidized; escrow stakes at bet time so the pool is real (the min-balance loss clamp can't apply at resolution without shorting the pot — cap the bet at `balance - minBalance` upfront instead); pari-mutuel makes the chip economy closed/zero-sum (today's fixed-odds bank mints and burns chips — leaderboard dynamics change); contestants may bet ON themselves (shortens their own odds, no moral hazard) but never against themselves (match-fixing). Rankings keep their jobs as matchmaker input and favorite/underdog labels; the pool only replaces the payout math.
